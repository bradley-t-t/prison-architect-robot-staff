Name                 "Robot Staff"
Author               Knight
Description          "Every member of your staff becomes a robot, and the ones that guard are built for the job. Drone Bays add flying Haulers that carry away the dead and Predators that shock prisoners caught misbehaving.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and load your prison. Every member of staff changes over at once, and new hires arrive as robots.\r\n[*]On an old save, build one Robot Uplink ($1,500) anywhere. Staff you already had may still be tired, hurt or corrupt from before. The uplink works through them a kind at a time, recharging any that run low and repairing and wiping clean the ones that guard.\r\n[*]For drones, zone a Drone Bay at least three tiles by three and build a Drone Charger ($3,000) in it. The charger builds one drone and keeps it charged.\r\n[*]Press the button on the charger to set the drone's job: Hauler, Predator on Patrol or Predator on Guard Post.\r\n[*]Hire, schedule and deploy your staff as you always have.\r\n[/olist]\r\n\r\n[h2]What you get[/h2]\r\nFour models, the Patrol, the Heavy, the Scout and the Enforcer, which the game deals out the way it deals out its own four guard builds. Every kind of staff wears its own colours and mark, so you can still tell a cook from a workman or the Warden at a glance. Guards wear guard blue, armed guards gunmetal with a belt of shells, dog handlers navy with a K-9 plate, workmen hi-vis, cooks a white apron, doctors a red cross, and the Warden a suit with a gold band.\r\n\r\n[h2]Drones[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]A Hauler flies the dead from anywhere in the prison to the nearest free morgue slab, the one a guard would have used. With Crematorium on, its machines count as slabs, so a Hauler can fly a body straight in. With no body waiting, a Hauler lifts rubbish within thirty tiles of its charger and takes it home to be broken down.\r\n[*]A Predator patrols within thirty tiles of its charger, or holds station beside it on Guard Post, and shocks a prisoner as soon as the game records them fighting, assaulting staff, killing, trying to escape or causing a serious injury. It wounds and stops, so it never finishes anybody, and it never goes after a prisoner for a record they already had.\r\n[*]Drones count as staff, so they go through staff doors, and they draw no wages.\r\n[*]A drone cannot be killed. Its charger keeps it repaired.\r\n[*]Flying drains the charge and the charger fills it back up. A drone that runs low breaks off and comes home, and a Hauler holding a body delivers it first.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]No robot tires, and no robot needs the toilet, the canteen or a rest.\r\n[*]Guards, armed guards and dog handlers are never corrupt, so bribes stop working on them.\r\n[*]Those same three cannot be killed, so a riot no longer costs you guards.\r\n[*]Speed, price, wages, equipment and research all stay the game's own. For every other kind of staff, only the look changes.\r\n[*]Stab vests still protect and are still listed on the guard. The mod stops drawing them, because a vest cut for a person looks wrong on a robot.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nThe mod redefines every kind of staff and ships its own staff needs, its own crooked guard settings and empty stab vest pictures. Turn off any other mod that changes how staff look or behave. Additional Guard Sprites is one."
