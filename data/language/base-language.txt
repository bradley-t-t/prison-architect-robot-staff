object_DroneCharger                    Drone Charger
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_DroneCharger    Builds one drone and keeps it charged. The button on the pad switches its drone between the two models. A Hauler carries the dead to the morgue and clears rubbish when there are no dead; a Predator patrols, or holds station on the pad, and shocks any prisoner it catches in misconduct. A drone works within thirty tiles of its pad, so put a pad in each part of the prison you want covered. It must stand in a Drone Bay.
