object_DroneCharger                    Drone Charger
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_DroneCharger    Builds one drone and keeps it charged. Press the button on the pad to switch the drone between Hauler, Predator on Patrol and Predator on Guard Post. A Hauler flies the dead from anywhere in the prison to the nearest free morgue slab, and clears rubbish within thirty tiles of its pad when there are no dead to move. A Predator patrols within thirty tiles of its pad, or holds station on it, and shocks any prisoner it catches in misconduct. It must stand in a Drone Bay.
